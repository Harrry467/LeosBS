THE IDEA

Better payment research can reveal where money is being lost, delayed or unnecessarily spent.

Proof Pay is intended as a framework for researching government payment systems. It can compare current costs with possible improvements and present the assumptions behind an estimate.

The goal is not to claim a saving before the evidence exists. The goal is to make the evidence easier to investigate.
